About

I am currently a Ph.D. student at Northwestern Polytechnical University, fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zhen Wang and co-advised by Dr. Qiaosheng Zhang at Shanghai AI Laboratory. Before that, I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees from Beihang University, where I was advised by Prof. Zhiguo Jiang . My research interests include graph learning, machine learning theory, multimodal large language models, and LLM safety.
